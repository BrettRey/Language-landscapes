Hi, Pairoj

Since you're starting with the glossary, here's how I'd frame it, because everything after it leans on the glossary.

For each load-bearing term, pin down the test that tells you when it applies. Determinative is defined by what the word does and where it sits, not by what it means, and the category/function distinction works the same way. A perfect Thai equivalent matters less than the test; some terms won't have one. Where Thai grammar already draws the same line, use its term. Where the nearest traditional term looks like a translation but merges a distinction the book keeps apart, that's the moment to coin something or keep the English term with a gloss, and flag it for me so we can talk it through.

Given that your students already draw CGEL-style trees, you'll feel where the traditional Thai terms pull against the analysis faster than I could point them out. Send me the tricky ones as they come.
