No rush on the publisher, and thank you again for taking this on.

When you do start, keep one thing in front of you, because it's where an edition like this stands or falls. Aim to preserve the distinctions the book is built on: category vs function, determinative vs determiner, count vs non-count. Don't reach for an Indonesian word for every term. Where Indonesian already draws the line, use the local term. Where the nearest traditional term looks right but merges two things the book keeps apart, keep the English term with a gloss instead, and say why.

You've already spotted exactly these kinds of distinctions in our back-and-forth about prenucleus and fronting, so I'm not worried about you here. It's the part a fluent machine pass can't do, and the part that makes the edition yours.
