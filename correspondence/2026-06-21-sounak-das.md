One more thought on terminology, since it's what you asked about and what actually matters.

When I say translate the terms, I mean preserve the distinctions. Finding a Bangla or Hindi word for every English one is not the goal. For each load-bearing term, ask whether the local tradition already draws the same line. Sometimes it does, and you use its word. Sometimes it has a word that looks right but merges a distinction the book keeps apart (determiner vs determinative has no standard translation anywhere, as I said), and then you're better off coining something or keeping the English term with a gloss. Protect the test that fixes the concept, not the word.

There's a nicer way to say why this is worth the care: a teacher who has no term for a distinction can't easily say the thing they already half-see. Handing them the term isn't pedantry, it gives them something to think with.
