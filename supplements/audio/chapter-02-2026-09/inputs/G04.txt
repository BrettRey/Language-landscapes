We don't need to pretend that the word “grammatical” has only one true meaning, but nor does it mean just anything. How do we establish the meaning of “grammatical”? As a comic in the chapter explains, what a word means depends on what we mean when we use it. That comic asks whether a hotdog is a sandwich, and it's reproduced in the written material.

So spend some time thinking about what “grammatical” and “ungrammatical” mean to you. Recall cases in which you've used them. What were you referring to? If none come to mind, consider creating some examples for each. Either way, try to think of commonalities that might generalize to other examples, especially if some borderline cases occur to you.
